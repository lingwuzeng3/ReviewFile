## How to define a good split
first we need to introdution the notion of entrophy
let $p_c$ be the proportion of data points labeled c in a node,
a node entropy is calc as follows
$$
s = -\sum_{c} p_c\log_2 p_c
$$
Low entropy means more predictable, high entropy means less predictable
