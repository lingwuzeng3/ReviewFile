# Drawing algorithm

##  DDA 
### 输入
$(x_1, y_1), (x_n, y_n)$ 
### 预处理步骤
$$
\begin{align*}
\Delta x = x_n - x_1 \\
\Delta y = y_n - y_1 \\
len = ( \text{max}(\Delta x, \Delta y))
\end{align*}
$$
### 算法
```python
i = 0
while i < len:
    if m < 1
        (x_k, y_k) -> (round(1+x_k), round(m + y_k))
    elif m == 1
        (x_k, y_k) -> (round(1+x_k), round(1 + y_k))
    else 
        (x_k, y_k) -> (round(1 / m + x_k), round(m + y_k))
    i += 1
```



## 中点画线法
### 输入
$(x_1, y_1), (x_n, y_n)$ 
### 预处理步骤
$$
\begin{align*}
\Delta x = x_n - x_1 \\
\Delta y = y_n - y_1 \\
D_{inital} = 2 \Delta y - \Delta x \\
\Delta D = 2 \Delta y - 2 \Delta x
\end{align*}
$$
### 算法
$$
\begin{align*}
    &\text{while not in end point: } \\
    & \qquad \text{if } D_{initial} < 0 \\  
    & \qquad \qquad (x_k, y_k) \to (x_{k} + 1, y_k)\\
    & \qquad \qquad D := D + 2 * \Delta y \\
    & \qquad else \\
    & \qquad \qquad (x_k, y_k) \to (x_{k} + 1, y_{k} + 1)\\
    & \qquad \qquad D := D + \Delta D \\
\end{align*}
$$

## Bresenham 算法
### 前提条件
- 直线斜率m 满足 $0 < m < 1$ 
- 在其他区域需要额外处理

### 输入
$(x_1, y_1), (x_n, y_n)$ 
### 预处理步骤
$$
\begin{align*}
\Delta x = x_n - x_1 \\
\Delta y = y_n - y_1 \\
P_{inital} = 2 \Delta y - \Delta x \\
\end{align*}
$$
### 算法
$$
\begin{align*}
    &\text{while not in end point: } \\
    & \qquad \text{if } P_{initial} < 0 \\  
    & \qquad \qquad (x_k, y_k) \to (x_{k} + 1, y_k)\\
    & \qquad \qquad D := D + 2 * \Delta y \\
    & \qquad else \\
    & \qquad \qquad (x_k, y_k) \to (x_{k}+ 1, y_{k} + 1)\\
    & \qquad \qquad D := D + 2 \cdot (\Delta y - \Delta x)   \\
\end{align*}
$$

## mid_point 画圆 算法
### 输入
$(x_0， y_0) \, \, R$  
### 预处理步骤
$$
\begin{align*}
x_k := 0 \, \, y_k = R \\
P_{inital} = 1 - R
\end{align*}
$$
### 算法
$$
\begin{align*}
    &\text{while } x_c < y_c  \\
    & \qquad \text{if } P_{initial} < 0 \\  
    & \qquad \qquad (x_k, y_k) \to (x_{k} + 1 , y_k)\\
    & \qquad \qquad P := P + 2 * x_k + 1\\
    & \qquad else \\
    & \qquad \qquad (x_k, y_k) \to (x_{k} + 1, y_{k} - 1)\\
    & \qquad \qquad P := P - 2* y_k + 2 * x_k + 1  \\
\end{align*}
$$
